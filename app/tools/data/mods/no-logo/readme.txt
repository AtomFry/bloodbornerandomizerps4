Bloodborne no-logo mod to remove the developer and publisher logos at game launch and when quitting out.  

The mod is very simple, it simply replaces the logo files with empty files, causing nothing to play at all.  This makes getting (back) to the game much faster.  The following files are patched:
CUSA03173-patch\dvdroot_ps4\menu\logo_fromsoft.gfx
CUSA03173-patch\dvdroot_ps4\menu\logo_jstudio.gfx
CUSA03173-patch\dvdroot_ps4\menu\logo_sce.gfx

This assumes you have a jailbroken PS4 and know how to make your own fpkg files.  Tested working with 720p60 patch by Lance McDonald.

1. Extract the dvdroot_ps4 folder into the CUSA03173-patch folder before making your pkg.
2. Patch as normal with pkg installer
